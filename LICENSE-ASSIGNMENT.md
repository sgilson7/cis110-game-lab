## What it covers

- The words of both versions of the page: `data/strings.json` and
  `data/tool/strings.json`.
- The schedule, rubric and limits in `data/schedule.json`.
- `GLOSSARY.md`.
- The Word, Excel and PowerPoint templates and the two example folders in
  `data/templates/`. These are generated by Builder's office-intake kit and are
  also available under Builder's MIT licence.
- The drawings in `data/img/art/`.
- The PDF and single-file HTML editions the build writes.

The code (`crates/`, `web/`, `scripts/`, `tools/` and `tests/`) is under the MIT licence in `LICENSE`.

## What it does not cover

The screenshots in `data/img/games/` show other projects as examples and are
not part of the assignment. `ape-race.jpg` is Peter's (ptonkle). `LICENSES.md`
lists each one.
